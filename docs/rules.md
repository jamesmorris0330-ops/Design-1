# Playing THE EXPERIMENT

THE EXPERIMENT is a continuous, real-time survival game for **1–8 players**. Create or join using a nickname and room code. Play alone, bring friends, or let the host add optional CPUs in the lobby. Everyone human marks ready; the host selects a starting shelter and chooses **Start survival**. Friends can take available CPU seats during play. See [CPU companions](cpu-players.md).

The goal is to keep one shared shelter standing while improving your community. The former Stability, Compliance, secret-directive, and finale rules apply only to older saved round-based games; they are not the rules of a new survival world.

## Move, fight, and collect

On desktop, use **WASD / arrows** to move, mouse to aim, hold click to shoot, **E** to collect supplies or rescue someone close by, and **R** to reload. On phones, use the left movement stick and right aim/fire stick, plus the Collect/Rescue and Reload buttons.

Pistols, rifles, and shotguns have different damage, range, firing speeds, and magazine sizes. Shots use ammunition. Reloading transfers reserve ammunition into the magazine; an empty magazine triggers a reload when reserves are available. The server decides movement, attacks, hits, damage, and loot.

Roaming infected attack survivors or the shelter. A first horde begins after 90 seconds of active play, followed by nightly attacks. Later hordes can include tougher brutes. Defeat the horde's enemies to finish its wave. Repair and restock before the next attack.

Supply caches contain wood, metal, cloth, electronics, ammunition, food, water, medicine, and sometimes weapons or gear. Only the collecting player receives the contents. Pack capacity limits collection; leftovers remain for another visit or player. Fully collected world caches replenish materials after time passes. Guns and gear are not endlessly regenerated by reopening one cache.

Food restores hunger, water restores thirst, and medicine restores health. Use them from Inventory when needed. Armor reduces infected damage, boots improve movement, and backpacks increase carrying capacity. Gear benefits apply while owned. At zero health you are downed; after a 20-second active-time recovery wait, choose the recovery action to return to the shelter with your inventory. Downed survivors cannot fight or collect. Treatment helps injured survivors who are still standing.

## Build one shelter

The host initially chooses one of five types:

| Shelter | Starting health | Defense | Recruited-survivor capacity |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Farmhouse | 1,100 | 16 | 6 |
| Bunker | 1,600 | 32 | 4 |
| Warehouse | 1,300 | 12 | 10 |
| Apartment | 950 | 20 | 8 |
| Ranger Station | 1,200 | 23 | 6 |

These capacities limit rescued NPCs, not human/CPU player seats. Everyone shares the active base; each player has a private pack. Shelter health reaching zero ends the expedition. The host can also end it explicitly. The persistent room lets everyone return to the lobby and start a fresh world together.

Return near the shelter to craft ammunition, medicine, guns, gear, and facilities, or to upgrade assets. Costs are paid when a project starts. Its visible timer advances with the survival clock; up to three jobs can run at once. Guns, gear, recruited survivors, facilities, and the shelter can reach level five. Higher levels require more materials. Workbenches speed projects, storage expands pack capacity, barricades improve defense, and rain collectors supply water.

Explore alternative shelter sites to discover them. Only the host can move the shared base, while near a discovered destination and outside an active horde. The destination must fit the recruited crew. Base levels, facilities, and rescued survivors relocate; the shelter keeps the same proportion of remaining health. Moving does not create a second base or provide free repairs.

## Rescue and improve survivors

Move close to a rescue marker and use Rescue. Recruitment costs two food and one medicine and requires space at the shelter.

- **Guardian:** fights infected around the base.
- **Medic:** heals injured players near the base.
- **Scavenger:** provides supplies to an available nearby player with pack space.
- **Engineer:** repairs the shelter over time.

Upgrades strengthen these contributions. Recruited survivors have illustrated portraits and public introductory dialogue; speech depends on the device's available voices. They differ from optional CPU player seats.

## Calendar goals

By default, one full game day equals **one real hour of active play**. The host can choose two or four hours in the lobby. The first day starts at 08:00, so its remaining portion is shorter than a full cycle. Seven game days form a week; thirty form a month.

Daily, weekly, and monthly goals track the whole community's scavenging, infected kills, rescues, upgrades, survived days, and defeated hordes. Each goal's reward can be claimed **once for the room**, and goes into the claiming player's private pack. Use communal supplies to share it. Goals refresh when their calendar period expires. Later periods substitute achievable goals when finite rescues or upgrades have been exhausted.

The world pauses when every human disconnects. Host pause also freezes the clock, needs, attacks, projects, and elections. Saved progress resumes after reconnection or server restart; real time spent offline does not create attacks or complete upgrades.

## Duties and elections

Open **Duties** to elect officers, inspect assignments, share supplies, and treat nearby injured survivors. Any active player can open an election for an unoccupied election slot. Elections last 30 seconds of active game time or finish sooner when all eligible players have voted. Each eligible survivor has one private, locked ballot and may vote for themselves. New arrivals wait for the next election; taking over an existing CPU keeps that seat's eligibility. CPUs vote too. A unique leading candidate wins; a tied or empty tally keeps the current officer.

| Elected position | Daily office duty |
| --- | --- |
| Captain | Claim one completed community goal |
| Defender | Defeat six infected |
| Scout | Fully collect three supply caches |
| Medic | Restore 40 health to survivors |
| Engineer | Finish two crafting or upgrade projects |

Every player also has a daily crew duty to contribute two points. Fully collecting a cache, finishing a project, or claiming a goal contributes one point; an infected kill contributes half a point; restoring 40 health contributes one point. Elected duties are additional. A player may hold more than one position, which also means more responsibilities.

Each completed duty adds **one food, one water, and six rounds** to the communal stash. At the next day boundary, each unfinished duty costs the shelter **20 health**. Elections transfer that office's existing daily progress rather than creating repeat rewards. Duties are public; individual ballots stay private.

Deposit or withdraw communal resources while near the shelter. Your private pack and carrying capacity still apply. Transfers do not earn duty credit. Use one medicine to treat a nearby injured player or recruited survivor, restoring up to 40 health; treatment cannot revive someone already downed.

## Sound, privacy, and host controls

Click **Enable sound** to allow ambient audio, shot/impact cues, public narration, and survivor voices. You can mute audio, turn voices off separately, and adjust volume. Dialogue remains visible without device voices. Private inventories and ballots are never automatically read aloud.

The server sends every browser the same public world and only that player's private inventory, needs, owned equipment, statistics, and active ballot. Spectators receive no private player view. The host has the same privacy limits and can pause/resume, transfer host, remove a player, mute chat, or end the expedition. Keeping the same browser profile and server address reclaims your player after refresh; a code and nickname alone do not reclaim someone else's identity.
