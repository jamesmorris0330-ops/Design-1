# Playing THE EXPERIMENT

Play with 1–8 humans in a group of 4–8 total Subjects. A solo host can mark ready and start with CPUs filling the remaining seats. New people joining during active play can replace available CPUs without resetting the Subject's score, dossier, or already locked choices. See [CPU players](cpu-players.md).

## Two conditions to qualify

There is no fixed traitor. To qualify, Group Stability must remain **above zero** and your own Compliance must reach the requirement in your private dossier. Several Subjects can qualify. Equal scores do not require an invented single winner.

Stability starts at 60 and cannot exceed 100. Your Compliance requirement is fixed when the game begins: ceiling of 1.5 × initial total rounds. Extending a game adds opportunities, never raises this target. Total rounds include exactly one finale.

## Ordinary Trial

Read the public crisis and your private directive. Discuss, then secretly distribute three units among Medical, Security, and Reserve. Medical and Security each need at least one unit per Subject in the Trial's frozen roster. Each met target adds 10 Stability; each missed target removes 15. Your directive offers 2 or 3 Compliance when its condition is fulfilled. Reserve gives no automatic personal points.

No response contributes no units and earns no directive reward. Individual allocations and Compliance remain private. The Trial reveal shows aggregate allocations and Stability changes.

The Trial then selects a vote:

- **Exposure:** reveal the unique leading target's completed directive.
- **Restriction:** the unique leading target must allocate at least one Medical unit in the next ordinary Trial.
- **Trust:** the unique leading target gains one Compliance. Trust vote totals determine final trust rankings.

You cannot target yourself. Abstention is permitted; a tied lead causes no mechanical effect. Ballots are private until consequences disclose them. Restrictions are used only when a compatible ordinary Trial remains.

## More rounds

If extensions are enabled, a checkpoint before the finale lets the host propose 1, 2, 3, or 5 extra Trials within the selected 15- or 20-round cap. A strict majority of Subjects must consent. Missing ballots count as no. Scores, history, and original requirements persist.

The checkpoint is offered consistently, so its appearance does not reveal anyone's private scores. Subjects can continue because a session feels short, their own Compliance is low, or public standings are tied.

## Finale

Every Subject gets a final statement, a private Final Directive, and a secret choice:

- **Protect the Group** contributes 40 divided by the starting Subject count to Stability.
- **Protect Yourself** removes 120 divided by that count.

All locked choices resolve together. Completing your Final Directive adds 3 Compliance. The server waits for every eligible Subject's final choice; its warning timer does not invent choices for absent players.

Resolution determines survival, then individual qualification. Full Reveal discloses the session's permitted hidden directives, decisions, and effects. The room persists for results and rematches. Stability collapse ends the game immediately with failure and reveal. Host termination marks the game aborted and does not publish still-hidden information.

## Privacy and moderation

Your dossier belongs to your anonymous browser session. The host cannot see your private state or edit scores. Pause freezes game actions and timers; chat can remain available. Host advance follows the same missing-action rules. Removing a Subject preserves locked inputs and history. If fewer than four Subjects remain, play pauses until restart or termination.
